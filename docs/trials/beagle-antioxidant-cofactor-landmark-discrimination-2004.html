Prior experience, antioxidants and mitochondrial cofactors in aged beagles (landmark discrimination)

This study fed aged dogs food providing higher levels of vitamin E and tested them on landmark-discrimination tasks. Higher dietary vitamin E produced higher serum vitamin E concentrations and improved landmark-discrimination performance, although factors other than vitamin E also contributed to the response. The published abstract gives no sample size, dose, duration or comparator.
