Ashwagandha root extract on gut parameters in healthy geriatric research Beagles

A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in Telangana, India randomly divided 12 healthy geriatric research Beagles aged 12-15 years, housed at a certified kennel, into ARE (KSM-66 ashwagandha root extract, 15 mg/kg orally once daily for 2 months; n = 6) and starch placebo (n = 6) groups, hypothesising that ARE would improve vital parameters for healthy ageing through the gut. Haematology, biochemistry, stool parameters and faecal metabolites were assessed on days 0, 30 and 60. ARE increased erythrocytes and haemoglobin, lowered ALT and AST at day 60 versus placebo, reduced faecal score, modulated L-citrulline and lowered propionic acid and total SCFAs from baseline.
