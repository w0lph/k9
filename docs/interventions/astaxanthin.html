Trials and cohorts in the canine aging trial registry

  • Dietary astaxanthin and age-associated mitochondrial dysfunction in young and geriatric Beagles (completed): Mitochondrial function improved in both young and geriatric dogs by increasing (P<0.05) ATP production, mitochondria mass, and cytochrome c oxidoreductase activity, especially in geriatric dogs compared with young dogs; astaxanthin also increased the reduced to oxidized glutathione ratio in young dogs and decreased nitric oxide in both age groups. Dietary astaxanthin improved mitochondrial function in blood leukocytes, most likely by alleviating oxidative damage to cellular DNA and protein.
