Products

  • Surolan® (NADA/ANADA NADA 141-298)

Surolan® NADA NADA 141-298, Original approval, November 23, 2009; sponsor Elanco US Inc.; species: Dogs; foi_id 862; FDA PDF

Indication

Treatment of canine otitis externa associated with susceptible strains of yeast (Malassezia pachydermatis) and bacteria (Staphylococcus pseudintermedius).

Dose regimen

5 drops, Otic (instilled in the ear canal), Twice daily, 7 consecutive days; ear cleaned and dried before treatment; eardrum verified intact

Instill 5 drops of SUROLAN in the ear canal twice daily and massage the ear. Therapy should continue for 7 consecutive days.

Target-animal safety

dose multiples 1X, 3X, 5X; duration 42 consecutive days of daily application (6X the recommended treatment duration); study period day -7 to day 56; animals 32 adult Beagles (twenty-four treated, 8 control; 4/sex/group).

A total of 32 adult Beagle dogs were used in this study. Twenty- four dogs were treated with SUROLAN and 8 dogs were used as a control. Body weights ranged from 8.84 to 16.38 kg. Four males and four females were randomly assigned to four groups (4/sex/group).

Effectiveness

GCP multi-site (31 clinics in the United States and Canada) randomized, active-controlled non-inferiority field study of SUROLAN (5 drops BID) vs OTOMAX (4 or 8 drops BID) for 7 consecutive days in dogs with otitis externa (Study #IO CC-0800, Oct 2001 to Nov 2002).; n = Three hundred thirty-seven enrolled (169 SUROLAN, 168 OTOMAX); 176 evaluated for effectiveness (91 SUROLAN, 85 OTOMAX); 322 for safety (161 per group); primary endpoint: Binary improvement vs no improvement in clinical score (pain/discomfort, swelling, redness, exudate and overall) 2-4 days after treatment; non-inferiority if one-sided lower 95% confidence limit of SUROLAN minus OTOMAX difference is no less than -10%; result: 96.7% of SUROLAN cases and 95.2% of OTOMAX cases had clinical improvement; overall lower 95% CL -3.0% (difference 1.4%); SUROLAN non-inferior to OTOMAX. Mean clinical score improvement 5.6 (SUROLAN) vs 5.5 (OTOMAX).

Three hundred and thirty-seven dogs (169 treated with SUROLAN and 168 treated with OTOMAX) were enrolled ranging in weight from 1.8 to 68.0 kg and age from less than 1 year to 20 years old. Of these, 176 (91 dogs treated with SUROLAN and 85 dogs treated with OTOMAX) were used to evaluate effectiveness and 322 dogs (161 treated with SUROLAN and 161 treated with OTOMAX) were used to evaluate safety.
