Products

  • Mometavet® (NADA/ANADA 200-536)

Mometavet® NADA 200-536, Original approval, May 17, 2016; sponsor Dechra Veterinary Products LLC; species: Dogs; foi_id 1225; FDA PDF

Indication

Treatment of otitis externa in dogs caused by susceptible strains of yeast (Malassezia pachydermatis) and bacteria (Pseudomonas spp. including P. aeruginosa, coagulase-positive staphylococci, Enterococcus faecalis, Proteus mirabilis and beta-hemolytic streptococci).

Dose regimen

4 drops from the 7.5 g, 15 g or 30 g containers (2 drops from the 215 g bottle) for dogs under 30 lbs, Topical (otic), into the ear canal after cleaning and drying; eardrum must be intact, once daily, 7 consecutive days; dogs weighing 30 lbs or more receive 8 drops (4 drops from the 215 g bottle).

For dogs weighing less than 30 lbs, instill 4 drops from the 7.5 g tubes and bottles, 15 g tubes and bottles, and 30 g bottles (2 drops from the 215 g bottle) once daily in the ear canal.
