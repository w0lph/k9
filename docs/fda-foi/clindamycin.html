Products

  • ClindaMed™ Oral Drops (NADA/ANADA 200-538)

ClindaMed™ Oral Drops NADA 200-538, Original approval, April 19, 2013; sponsor Bimeda Animal Health Ltd.; species: Dogs and cats; foi_id 1226; FDA PDF

Indication

Dogs: skin infections (wounds and abscesses) due to coagulase positive staphylococci; deep wounds and abscesses, dental infections and osteomyelitis due to Bacteroides fragilis, Prevotella melaninogenicus, Fusobacterium necrophorum, Clostridium perfringens (and S. aureus). Cats: skin infections, deep wounds/abscesses and dental infections due to the listed organisms.

Dose regimen

2.5-15.0 mg/lb body weight (dogs: infected wounds, abscesses, dental infections), Oral, Every 12 hours, Up to a maximum of 28 days if clinical judgment indicates; stop after three or four days if no response. Osteomyelitis (dogs): 5.0-15.0 mg/lb every 12 hours for a minimum of 28 days. Cats: 5.0-15.0 mg/lb once every 24 hours, max 14 days.

Dogs: Infected Wounds, Abscesses, and Dental Infections Oral: 2.5-15.0 mg/lb body weight every 12 hours. Duration: Treatment may be continued up to a maximum of 28 days if clinical judgment indicates.
