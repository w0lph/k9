Products

  • DuOtic® (NADA/ANADA 141-579)

DuOtic® NADA 141-579, Original approval, March 15, 2024; sponsor Dechra Veterinary Products LLC; species: Dogs; foi_id 15165; FDA PDF

Indication

Treatment of otitis externa in dogs associated with susceptible strains of yeast (Malassezia pachydermatis)

Dose regimen

1 tube (1 mL) per affected ear, Otic (external ear canal), twice, 7 days apart, administered by a veterinary professional; do not clean the ear canal for 45 days after the initial administration

Administer one dose (1 tube) per affected ear and repeat administration in 7 days.

Effectiveness

Randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled, multi-center field study (US and Spain; Study D20028, GLP); 1:1 DuOtic 1 mL per affected ear vs saline on Days 0 and 7; 239 dogs enrolled (120 DuOtic, 119 placebo); n = 197 (102 DuOtic, 95 placebo) in effectiveness evaluation; primary endpoint: Treatment success on Day 45: Overall Severity Score ≤3 in the evaluable ear with no worsening of any individual clinical sign vs Day 0; result: Success rate 62.31% DuOtic vs 17.84% placebo (back-transformed least squares estimates); p = 0.0001

A total of 197 dogs (102 DuOtic™ and 95 placebo control) were included in the effectiveness evaluation. The success rate on Day 45 in the DuOtic™ treated group was 62.31% versus 17.84% for the placebo control group
