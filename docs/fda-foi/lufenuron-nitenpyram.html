Products

  • Program® Flavor Tabs® and Capstar® Flea Management System™ (NADA/ANADA 141-205)

Program® Flavor Tabs® and Capstar® Flea Management System™ NADA 141-205, Original approval, June 11, 2003; sponsor Elanco US Inc.; species: Dogs and Cats; foi_id 723; FDA PDF

Indication

Concurrent use of PROGRAM Flavor Tabs and CAPSTAR Tablets in dogs and puppies (and in cats and kittens) to kill adult fleas and prevent flea eggs from hatching.

Dose regimen

Dogs: 1 tablet each by body-weight band: CAPSTAR 11.4 mg (2-25 lb) or 57.0 mg (26-125 lb) nitenpyram; PROGRAM 45.0, 90.0, 204.9 or 409.8 mg lufenuron, oral, CAPSTAR once or twice weekly (if adult fleas are seen); PROGRAM once per month, dogs over 90 lb receive the appropriate combination of PROGRAM Flavor Tabs; separate cat table (2-6 lb 90 mg, 7-15 lb 204.9 mg lufenuron with 11.4 mg nitenpyram)

Effectiveness

Three-group, multi-center, masked, placebo-controlled field study (11 sites) of nitenpyram tablets alone or with PROGRAM Flavor Tabs for rapid flea removal in 273 dogs and 265 cats; live/dead/moribund fleas counted 4-6 hours after dosing at two visits 7-14 days apart.; n = 113; primary endpoint: Percent dead and moribund fleas within six hours of administration; result: Dogs visit 1: nitenpyram 98.6% (n=108), nitenpyram + PROGRAM 98.5% (n=113) vs placebo 6.2% (n=52); visit 2: 99.1% and 99.3% vs 13.1%. Cats: 98.4%/98.7% vs 5.7% at visit 1; 99.9%/99.7% at visit 2. Conclusion: nitenpyram alone or with PROGRAM >98% effective within 6 hours.

Nitenpyram + PROGRAM® 98.5% (n=113) 99.3% (n=82) 98.7% (n=108) 99.7% (n=44)
