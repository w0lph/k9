Products

  • Sentinel® Spectrum® (NADA/ANADA 141-333)

Sentinel® Spectrum® NADA 141-333, Original approval, December 08, 2011; sponsor Intervet, Inc.; species: Dogs; foi_id 891; FDA PDF

Indication

Prevention of heartworm disease (Dirofilaria immitis); prevention and control of flea populations (Ctenocephalides felis); treatment and control of adult roundworm, hookworm, whipworm and tapeworm (Taenia pisiformis, Echinococcus multilocularis, E. granulosus) infections in dogs and puppies 2 lb or more and 6 weeks of age and older.

Dose regimen

0.5 mg/kg (0.23 mg/lb) milbemycin oxime, 10 mg/kg (4.55 mg/lb) lufenuron and 5 mg/kg (2.28 mg/lb) praziquantel (minimum), oral (chewable tablet), once a month, for heartworm prevention, give monthly from within 1 month of first seasonal mosquito exposure until at least 6 months after last exposure

SENTINEL SPECTRUM is given orally, once a month, at the minimum dosage of 0.23 mg/pound body weight (0.5 mg/kg) of milbemycin oxime, 4.55 mg/pound (10 mg/kg) of lufenuron, and 2.28 mg/pound (5 mg/kg) of praziquantel.

Target-animal safety

dose multiples 1X, 3X, 5X; duration every two weeks for seven treatments (90-day study) in 10-week-old Beagle puppies; doses 2.5/50.7/25.1, 7.5/152.1/75.3 and 12.5/253.5/125.5 mg/kg milbemycin/lufenuron/praziquantel; animals 20 males and 20 females.

Forty purpose-bred beagles (20 males and 20 females), approximately ten weeks of age at the time of treatment, were used in this study.

Effectiveness

Pivotal laboratory dose-confirmation study NAH-08-0008 (GCP): 16 Beagles inoculated with 50 D. immitis L3 larvae on Day -30; 8 control (multi-vitamin) and 8 SENTINEL SPECTRUM-treated dogs dosed on Days 0, 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150; adult worm counts at necropsy Day 181.; primary endpoint: Percent reduction in geometric mean adult heartworm count vs control; result: Control geometric mean 18.8 worms (range 0-39); treated 0.0; 100.0% effectiveness (p = 0.0002).

SENTINEL SPECTRUM is 100% effective against experimental infection with Dirofilaria immitis when administered once per month for 6 consecutive months after laboratory infection.

Sentinel® Spectrum® NADA 141-333, Supplemental approval, September 28, 2017; sponsor Intervet, Inc.; species: Dogs; foi_id 2282; FDA PDF

Indication

Supplement adds treatment and control of adult tapeworm (Dipylidium caninum) infections in dogs and puppies 2 lb or more and 6 weeks of age and older.

Dose regimen

0.5 mg/kg (0.23 mg/lb) milbemycin oxime, 10 mg/kg (4.55 mg/lb) lufenuron and 5 mg/kg (2.28 mg/lb) praziquantel (minimum), oral (chewable tablet), once every month, administer immediately after or with a normal meal; for heartworm prevention give monthly for at least 6 months after mosquito exposure

SENTINEL SPECTRUM should be administered orally, once every month, at the minimum dosage of 0.23 mg/lb (0.5 mg/kg) milbemycin oxime, 4.55 mg/lb (10 mg/kg) lufenuron, and 2.28 mg/lb (5 mg/kg) praziquantel.

Effectiveness

Laboratory dose confirmation study NAH-14-093 (GLP): 16 dogs experimentally infected with US-derived D. caninum via infected fleas; 8 treated once with SENTINEL SPECTRUM (minimum 5 mg/kg praziquantel) on Day 82 and 8 untreated controls; necropsy Day 92. Supported by non-interference study NAH-02-0032 (29 naturally infected dogs, non-final tablet formulation, 100% effective).; n = 8; primary endpoint: Percent reduction in geometric mean tapeworm count vs control; result: 100% effective (treated geometric mean 0.0 vs control 30.7, range 8-124; p < 0.0001).
