Topical treatment of conjunctivitis caused by susceptible bacteria in dogs and cats.

Indication

Topical treatment of conjunctivitis caused by susceptible bacteria in dogs and cats.

Approximately ½ inch strip of ointment, Topical, ophthalmic (to the affected eye(s)), 2 to 4 times a day

Effectiveness
