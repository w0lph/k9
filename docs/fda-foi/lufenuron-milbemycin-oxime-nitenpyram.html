  • Sentinel® Flavor Tabs® and Capstar® Flea Management System™ (NADA/ANADA 141-204)

Sentinel® Flavor Tabs® and Capstar® Flea Management System™ NADA 141-204, Original approval, June 11, 2003; sponsor Intervet, Inc.; species: Dogs; foi_id 722; FDA PDF

Indication

Concurrent use of SENTINEL Flavor Tabs and CAPSTAR Tablets in dogs and puppies to kill adult fleas and prevent flea eggs from hatching.

Dose regimen

1 tablet of each by body-weight band: CAPSTAR 11.4 mg or 57.0 mg nitenpyram; SENTINEL 46 mg/2.3 mg up to 460 mg/23.0 mg lufenuron/milbemycin oxime, oral, CAPSTAR once or twice weekly (only if adult fleas are seen); SENTINEL once per month, products are not co-packaged; dogs over 100 lb receive the appropriate combination of SENTINEL tablets

Target-animal safety

dose multiples 5X; duration 90 days (nitenpyram 5X once daily; milbemycin oxime/lufenuron 5X once every two weeks, 7 treatments); animals 16.

Animals: 16 beagle dogs (8 male and 8 female), 10 to 11 weeks of age at start of dosing

Effectiveness

Laboratory non-interference / confirmation study in a simulated home environment: 72 Beagle dogs in eight groups of nine (untreated control, CAPSTAR twice weekly, CAPSTAR once weekly, SENTINEL only on days 0/30/58, SENTINEL + CAPSTAR once weekly; three Program groups reported under NADA 141-205); weekly flea counts days 14-63.; n = 72; primary endpoint: Percent effectiveness (reduction in adult flea counts vs untreated control) by day; result: CAPSTAR twice weekly >90% from Day 14; CAPSTAR once weekly >90% from Day 21; SENTINEL + CAPSTAR once weekly >90% from Day 28; SENTINEL alone >90% from Day 35. Concurrent use gave greater reduction in adult fleas than SENTINEL alone.

Animals : 72 Beagle dogs (41 males and 31 females), adults, > 6 months of age, between 14.1 and 35.2 pounds.
