Products

  • Vercom Paste Anthelmintic (NADA/ANADA 133-953)

Vercom Paste Anthelmintic NADA 133-953, Supplemental approval, September 12, 1991; sponsor Elanco US Inc.; species: Dogs and Cats; foi_id 1635; FDA PDF

Indication

Removal of nematode (and cestode) parasites: in dogs and puppies hookworms (Ancylostoma caninum, Uncinaria stenocephala), ascarids (Toxocara canis, Toxocara leonina), whipworms (Trichuris vulpis) and tapeworms (Dipylidium caninum, Taenia pisiformis); in cats and kittens hookworms (A. tubaeforme), ascarids (T. cati) and tapeworms (D. caninum, T. taeniaeformis) - unchanged by this supplement

Dose regimen

10 mg febantel and 1.0 mg praziquantel/kg BW (1 g paste per 7.5 lb) for mature dogs and cats, Oral; directly into the mouth or, for mature animals, mixed with food, Daily, 3 consecutive days. Puppies and kittens younger than 6 months: 1 g/5 lb (15 mg febantel and 1.5 mg praziquantel/kg) daily for 3 consecutive days on a full stomach

Dogs and Cats: 1 g/7.5 lb body weight (BW) given to mature dogs and cats (10 mg febantel and 1.0 mg praziquantel/kg BW) given daily for 3 consecutive days.

Target-animal safety

The addition of this warning is based on data gathered during the safety evaluation of febantel alone for Rintal Tabs, NADA 140-912.
FindingQuote
At elevated doses, toxicity may be increased in animals with pre-existing hepatic or renal disease (basis for the new label warning; data from the Rintal Tabs febantel-alone safety evaluation in cats)It was shown in this study, that, at elevated doses, toxicity may be increased in the animal already compromised by hepatic or renal disease.
