  • Trimeprazine with Prednisolone (NADA/ANADA 200-784)

Trimeprazine with Prednisolone NADA 200-784, Original approval, June 20, 2024; sponsor ZyVet Animal Health, Inc.; species: Dogs; foi_id 15546; FDA PDF

Indication

Antipruritic (relief of itching and inflammation associated with skin disorders of dogs) and antitussive (kennel cough/tracheobronchitis, bronchitis, and coughs of nonspecific origin).

Dose regimen

Up to 10 lb: ½ tablet; 11-20 lb: 1 tablet; 21-40 lb: 2 tablets; over 40 lb: 3 tablets, Oral, Twice daily (initial dosage), After 4 days reduce to half the initial dose or an amount sufficient to maintain remission; each tablet contains 5 mg trimeprazine (as tartrate) and 2 mg prednisolone.

Effectiveness

Pivotal blood-level bioequivalence study (Study No. 113-BC-3422): masked, randomized, four-period, two-sequence, single oral dose crossover in healthy fasted dogs, generic vs Temaril-P tablets, 14-day washout.; n = 28; primary endpoint: CMAX and AUC for trimeprazine (reference-scaled average bioequivalence) and prednisolone (average bioequivalence, 90% CI within 0.80-1.25); result: Bioequivalence demonstrated for both analytes (prednisolone AUC ratio 0.94, CMAX ratio 1.01; trimeprazine AUC ratio 1.04, CMAX ratio 1.13 with 95% upper bounds < 0).

The in vivo blood-level study was conducted in 28 healthy, fasted dogs.
