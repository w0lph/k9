Products

  • Task® Tabs (NADA/ANADA 048-271)

Task® Tabs NADA 048-271, Supplemental approval, March 04, 1999; sponsor Cronus Pharma Specialities India Private Ltd.; species: Dogs, puppies, cats and kittens (supplement adds kittens not less than three months of age and adult dogs); foi_id 311; FDA PDF

Indication

Removal and control of roundworms (Toxocara canis, Toxocara cati, Toxascaris leonina) and hookworms (Ancylostoma caninum, Ancylostoma tubaeforme, Uncinaria stenocephala) in dogs, puppies, cats and kittens.

Dose regimen

5 mg dichlorvos per pound body weight, oral (tablet), single dose

TASK® Tabs are administered as a single dose to provide 5 mg dichlorvos per pound body weight to dogs, puppies, cats and kittens.

Target-animal safety

dose multiples 1X (250 mg once), 2X (250 mg twice in one day), 5X (500 mg once); duration single day; animals 15.

This study included 15 dogs over 60 pounds. Five geriatric dogs (over 7 years of age) were included in the study. Ten adult dogs (between 1 and 7 years of age) were included in the study.

Effectiveness

Multi-investigator (14 sites) clinical field study of 5 mg/lb single oral dose in adult dogs; eggs-per-gram reduction and parasite clearance rates; plus a critical (necropsy) test in 25 young adult Beagles and a 12-site field study in 43 kittens 3-5 months old; n = 171 (roundworm infections); 124 (hookworm infections); primary endpoint: Parasite clearance rate (egg counts to zero) and EPG reduction rate; result: Dogs: roundworms cleared 157/171 (91.2%), EPG 549 to 17 (96.9%); hookworms cleared 114/124 (91.9%), EPG 348 to 16 (95.4%). Critical test: T. canis 141/141 (100%), T. leonina 213/292 (73%), A. caninum 147/151 (97.4%) expelled. Kittens: 91% clearance for both roundworms and hookworms

In this study, 171 dogs were treated for roundworm infections and 124 dogs were treated for hookworm infections.
