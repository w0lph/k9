Products

  • Kesium® (NADA/ANADA 200-749)

Kesium® NADA 200-749, Original approval, February 22, 2024; sponsor Ceva Sante Animale; species: Dogs and cats; foi_id 15070; FDA PDF

Indication

Dogs: skin and soft tissue infections (wounds, abscesses, cellulitis, superficial/juvenile and deep pyoderma) due to susceptible Staphylococcus aureus (beta-lactamase producing and non-producing), Staphylococcus spp., Streptococcus spp. and E. coli; periodontal infections due to susceptible aerobic and anaerobic bacteria. (Cats: skin/soft tissue infections and urinary tract infections.)

Dose regimen

6.25 mg/lb body weight, Oral, Twice a day, Skin/soft tissue and periodontal infections 5-7 days or 48 hours after symptoms subside; deep pyoderma may require 21 days; maximum 30 days

Dogs: The recommended dosage is 6.25 mg/lb of body weight twice a day.

Effectiveness

Blood-level bioequivalence study in dogs (US/BEQ/C662/2006), labelled as such: randomized, masked, four-period, two-sequence, single-dose crossover of generic 125 mg Kesium® vs RLNAD 125 mg CLAVAMOX® CHEWABLE in fasted intact male beagles, 7-day washout; average bioequivalence for amoxicillin, RSABE for clavulanic acid; n = 22; primary endpoint: CMAX and AUC (90% CI of geometric mean ratio within 0.80-1.25; RSABE criteria for clavulanic acid); result: Bioequivalent: amoxicillin AUC ratio 1.03 (0.98-1.08), CMAX 1.02 (0.95-1.10); clavulanic acid AUC ratio 0.96, CMAX 0.99 with 95% upper bounds < 0

Study Animals: 22 intact male beagle dogs, approximately 15 months to 4 years of age
