Products

  • Zeuterin™ (NADA/ANADA 141-217)

Zeuterin™ NADA 141-217, Original approval, March 17, 2003; sponsor Ark Sciences, Inc.; species: Dogs; foi_id 748; FDA PDF

Indication

Chemical sterilization in 3 to 10 month old male dogs

Dose regimen

0.2 to 1.0 mL per testicle (2.6 to 13.1 mg zinc) by testicular width: 10-12 mm 0.2 mL; 13-15 mm 0.3 mL; 16-18 mm 0.5 mL; 19-21 mm 0.7 mL; 22-24 mm 0.8 mL; 25-27 mm 1.0 mL, Intratesticular injection, One injection per testicle (single treatment), Dose per testicle chosen from testicular width measured at its widest point with a metric (mm) caliper; each mL contains 13.1 mg zinc as zinc gluconate

One injection is administered per testicle. Dose per testicle is based on testicular width as determined by measuring each testicle at its widest point using a metric scale (mm) caliper. Target-animal safety

dose multiples 1X, 1.5X, 2X; duration Injections on Days 0 and 14 (planned third injection on Day 28 not administered because testicles were too hard and small to inject); 2 month study with necropsy at Week 8; animals 24 male Beagle dogs, 6 months of age (four groups incl. placebo control).

Effectiveness

Multi-site (Arizona, Florida, Missouri, New York) clinical field study with historical control (semen analyses of normal intact male dogs). 270 male dogs of various breeds, 2.5-10 months old, testicular width 10-27 mm, received a single intratesticular injection per testicle dosed by width (0.2-1.0 mL); 225 completed; dogs followed at least 6 months; n = 224 (with Month 6 semen analysis); primary endpoint: Treatment success at Month 6: aspermia, azoospermia, necrospermia or oligospermia (<20 million sperm/mL); failure: 80% progressive forward motility and >20 million sperm/mL; result: Effectiveness 99.6%: of 224 dogs, one treatment failure; 171 aspermic (76.3%), 50 azoospermic (22.3%), 1 necrospermic (0.5%), 1 oligospermic (0.5%)

Semen was collected from 224 dogs at Month 6. Based on the criteria above, the effectiveness was 99.6%. Of the 224 dogs, there was one treatment failure, 171 dogs were aspermic (76.3%), 50 were azoospermic (22.3%), 1 was necrospermic (0.5%), and 1 was oligospermic (0.5%).

Of 270 dogs injected, 225 completed (28 not returned by owners, 10 castrated, 5 died, 2 euthanized); 224 had a Month 6 semen analysis; two Month-6 successes showed increased sperm counts at Month 12. Target animal safety was a GLP study at 1X/1.5X/2X by volume (6 dogs per group, placebo = bacteriostatic water) with injections on Days 0 and 14 (Day 28 injection not feasible); doses reflected an earlier version of the dose table. Also reported but not captured as separate records: a 16-month pilot (27 Beagles, 0.2 and 0.5 mL) and a 2-year dose determination study (40 Beagles, 0.25/0.30/0.35 mL; 0.3 mL chosen for 13 mm testicle).
