  • Halothane, USP (NADA/ANADA 200-200)

Halothane, USP NADA 200-200, Original approval, April 10, 1997; sponsor Halocarbon Products Corp.; species: Dogs, cats, and other non-food animals.; foi_id 1428; FDA PDF

Indication

Induction and maintenance of general anesthesia in dogs, cats and other non-food animals.

Dose regimen

Induction: usually between 2% and 5% inhaled concentration, Inhalation, Continuous during anesthesia (vaporizer concentration adjusted to effect), Maintenance usually 0.5% to 2%; lower concentrations needed with oxygen/nitrous oxide, tranquilizer pre-medication or barbiturates

The concentration of Halothane, USP required to induce anesthesia will vary from patient to patient but will usually be between 2% and 5%.

Effectiveness

ANADA (generic of Fort Dodge Halothane, USP, NADA 14-170): bioequivalence established by chemical equivalence; in vivo bioequivalence waived; no effectiveness study conducted; result: Chemical equivalence accepted in lieu of in vivo bioequivalence testing
